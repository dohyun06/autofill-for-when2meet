# Privacy Policy — When2meet Calendar Fill

- The extension stores the iCal addresses you enter only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They are never sent anywhere except to calendar.google.com to download your calendar.
- Calendar data is processed locally in your browser to find free 15-minute slots. Event titles and details are not stored or transmitted.
- The only thing sent to when2meet.com is your availability (which slots are free/busy), exactly as if you painted the grid by hand.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party servers, no account sign-in.
