# Privacy Policy — Autofill for When2meet

- The extension stores the iCal addresses you enter only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They are never sent anywhere except to the calendar server in the address itself (e.g. calendar.google.com), to download your calendar. The extension asks for access to that server only when you first use the address.
- Calendar data is processed locally in your browser to find free 15-minute slots. Event titles and details are not stored or transmitted.
- The only thing sent to when2meet.com is your availability (which slots are free/busy), exactly as if you painted the grid by hand.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party servers, no account sign-in.
